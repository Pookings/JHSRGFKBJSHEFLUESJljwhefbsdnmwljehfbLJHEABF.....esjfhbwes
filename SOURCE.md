# Source and attribution

Shiruh is an adaptation of Gen Chem Atlas by amirlobel-elinor.

Upstream repository: https://github.com/amirlobel-elinor/genchem-atlas
Upstream revision: 6373d497d012d494a85aaefd7e4617e6bd594122

The site owner has confirmed permission to reuse the source and content. No new blanket license is asserted by this adaptation. Original textbook references and source mappings are retained in the application.

The original dataset contains 30 units, 330 questions, 38 worked examples and 118 elements. The interactive lab implementation is retained. Changes include Shiruh branding, separate content files, a form-based content editor, removal of the upstream analytics integration, and removal of the upstream feedback email destination.


## Biology

## Portal and Element Atlas

The subject picker layout is adapted from https://github.com/amirlobel-elinor/premed-atlas. The element app is adapted from https://github.com/amirlobel-elinor/element-atlas. Reuse permission was confirmed by the site owner. Local subject navigation, Shiruh branding and noindex metadata were added, upstream analytics removed, and Element Atlas was bundled into a single page with its original learning code and data.
